Response & Containment Checklist

01
Isolate & Quarantine: Do not click hyperlinks, download attachments, or reply to the sender.
02
Verify via Official Channels: If this claims to be from a known service or financial institution, contact them directly through their official website or phone number.
03
Report to Security / IT: Forward the raw email headers and extracted domain signatures to your security or IT administrator.
04
Reset Credentials if Interacted: If you entered credentials or visited the link, immediately reset passwords from a secure device and activate MFA.
